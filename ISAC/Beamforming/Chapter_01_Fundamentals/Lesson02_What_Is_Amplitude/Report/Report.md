## Description

This report accompanies **Lesson 02** of the *Beamforming From First Principles Using MATLAB* educational project.

The report introduces the concept of **amplitude**, explains its mathematical role in a sinusoidal waveform, and demonstrates its effect through MATLAB simulations.

This lesson establishes the foundation for understanding signal magnitude before studying frequency, phase, time delay, and beamforming techniques.

---

## Learning Objectives

After completing this lesson, readers will be able to:

* Understand the meaning of signal amplitude.
* Explain how amplitude affects a sinusoidal waveform.
* Distinguish amplitude from frequency and phase.
* Generate and compare sinusoidal signals with different amplitudes using MATLAB.
* Interpret the effect of amplitude through graphical visualization.

## Educational Philosophy

> **Understand the concept, not memorize the equation.**

This lesson emphasizes conceptual understanding by isolating the effect of amplitude while keeping all other signal parameters unchanged. Students first develop an intuitive understanding of amplitude before exploring more advanced signal properties.
