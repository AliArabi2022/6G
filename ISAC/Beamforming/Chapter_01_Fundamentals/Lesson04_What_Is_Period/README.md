# Lesson 04 — What Is Period?

> *The period tells us how long it takes for a waveform to complete one full cycle.*

---

## Introduction

Welcome to the fourth lesson of the **Beamforming From First Principles Using MATLAB** educational project.

In the previous lesson, we learned that frequency describes how many cycles a waveform completes in one second. In this lesson, we introduce **period**, which describes the time required for one complete cycle.

You will learn the relationship between period and frequency, understand how to measure the duration of a cycle, and visualize the concept using MATLAB.

The concepts presented in this lesson are fundamental for understanding phase, time delay, wavelength, and beamforming.

---

## Why This Lesson Matters

Period is one of the fundamental time-domain properties of a signal.

It is directly related to:

* Waveform repetition
* Oscillation timing
* Signal analysis
* Radar pulse timing
* Wireless communication systems
* Digital signal processing
* Electromagnetic wave propagation
* Beamforming applications

Understanding period helps explain how signals evolve over time and how it is mathematically connected to frequency.

---

## Learning Question

> **What is the period of a sinusoidal wave, and how is it related to frequency?**

---

## Learning Objectives

After completing this lesson, you should be able to:

* Explain the meaning of period.
* Define the unit of period.
* Describe the relationship between period and frequency.
* Calculate the period from a known frequency.
* Measure one complete cycle on a waveform.
* Visualize the period of a sinusoidal signal using MATLAB.
