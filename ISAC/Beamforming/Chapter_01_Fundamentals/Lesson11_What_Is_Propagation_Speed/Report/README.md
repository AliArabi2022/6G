## Description

This report accompanies **Lesson 11** of the *Beamforming From First Principles Using MATLAB* educational project.

The report introduces the concept of **propagation speed**, derives the fundamental relationship between propagation speed, frequency, and wavelength, and demonstrates why changing the propagation medium affects wavelength while leaving the frequency unchanged.

---

## Learning Objectives

After completing this lesson, readers will be able to:

* Understand the physical meaning of propagation speed.
* Explain the relationship between propagation speed, frequency, and wavelength.
* Distinguish between source-controlled and medium-controlled wave properties.
* Calculate wavelength for different propagation speeds.
* Explain why propagation speed is fundamental in antenna engineering and beamforming.
