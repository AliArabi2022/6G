# Lesson 11 — What Is Propagation Speed?

> *The source determines the frequency, the medium determines the propagation speed, and the wavelength adjusts automatically.*

---

## Introduction

Welcome to the eleventh lesson of the **Beamforming From First Principles Using MATLAB** educational project.

In the previous lesson, we learned how waves propagate through space. This lesson investigates **how fast** they propagate and introduces one of the most important relationships in wave physics:

[
v=f\lambda
]

Understanding this relationship is essential before studying wavefronts, plane waves, antenna arrays, and beamforming.

---

## Why This Lesson Matters

Propagation speed is fundamental to:

* Wireless communication systems
* Radar systems
* Satellite communications
* Optical communications
* Acoustics
* Electromagnetic wave theory
* Antenna engineering
* Beamforming

Understanding propagation speed explains why wavelength changes while frequency remains constant when a wave enters a different medium.

---

## Learning Question

> **What is propagation speed, and how does it affect wavelength without changing the frequency?**

---

## Learning Objectives

After completing this lesson, you should be able to:

* Explain the concept of propagation speed.
* Describe the relationship between propagation speed, frequency, and wavelength.
* Distinguish between properties determined by the source and those determined by the propagation medium.
* Calculate wavelength for different propagation speeds.
* Explain why propagation speed is fundamental to antenna engineering.
