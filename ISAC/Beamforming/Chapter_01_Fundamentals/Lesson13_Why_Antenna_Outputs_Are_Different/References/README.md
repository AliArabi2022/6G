# References

This folder contains the scientific references, textbooks, journal papers, and educational resources used in Lesson 13.

## Recommended References

1. Constantine A. Balanis, *Antenna Theory: Analysis and Design*, 4th Edition.
2. David K. Cheng, *Field and Wave Electromagnetics*.
3. Matthew N. O. Sadiku, *Elements of Electromagnetics*.
4. Harry L. Van Trees, *Optimum Array Processing*.
5. Robert J. Mailloux, *Phased Array Antenna Handbook*.

## Notes

The concepts introduced in this lesson are fundamental to antenna arrays, direction-of-arrival estimation, phased-array systems, radar, wireless communications, and beamforming. These references provide additional theoretical background and practical insights for readers who wish to explore the subject in greater depth.
