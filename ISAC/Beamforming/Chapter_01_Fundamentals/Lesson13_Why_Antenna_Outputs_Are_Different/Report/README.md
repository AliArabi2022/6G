## Description

This report explains the physical origin of the differences observed in the outputs of antenna array elements, even though all antennas receive the same transmitted signal.

The lesson develops the concept from first principles by connecting wave propagation, propagation distance, arrival time, time delay, and phase difference. MATLAB simulations are used throughout the lesson to provide intuitive visualizations before introducing the underlying mathematical relationships.

The report concludes by explaining why phase differences are the fundamental source of spatial information exploited by beamforming algorithms.

---

## Main Topics

- Physical intuition behind antenna array observations
- Plane-wave propagation
- Propagation distance
- Arrival time
- Time delay
- Phase difference
- MATLAB visualization
- Relationship to beamforming

---

## Learning Outcome

After studying this report, readers should understand why different antennas observe different versions of the same transmitted signal and why these phase differences form the foundation of modern beamforming techniques.
