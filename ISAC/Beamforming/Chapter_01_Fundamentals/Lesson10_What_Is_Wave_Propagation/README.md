# Lesson 10 — What Is Wave Propagation?

> *A wave is more than a waveform—it is a disturbance that travels through space, carrying energy and information.*

---

## Introduction

Welcome to the tenth lesson of the **Beamforming From First Principles Using MATLAB** educational project.

In the previous lessons, we studied the fundamental properties of sinusoidal signals and introduced wavelength as the spatial description of a wave.

This lesson takes the next logical step by introducing **wave propagation**—the process by which waves travel through space and transport energy and information.

Understanding wave propagation is essential before studying propagation speed, plane waves, antenna arrays, and beamforming.

---

## Why This Lesson Matters

Wave propagation is fundamental to:

* Wireless communication systems
* Radar systems
* Satellite communications
* GPS navigation
* Electromagnetic wave theory
* Antenna arrays
* Beamforming

Every wireless technology relies on waves propagating through space.

---

## Learning Question

> **What is wave propagation, and how does a wave travel through space without transporting matter?**

---

## Learning Objectives

After completing this lesson, you should be able to:

* Explain the concept of wave propagation.
* Distinguish between oscillation and propagation.
* Understand that waves transport energy and information rather than matter.
* Interpret the travelling-wave equation.
* Explain why wave propagation is fundamental to communications and radar.

2. Navigate to the **MATLAB** folder.
3. Open

```text

```

4. Click **Run**.

6. Compare the displayed wave parameters in the MATLAB Command Window.

Running the MATLAB script will produce:
