# Lesson 01 — What Is a Sinusoidal Wave?

> *The first step toward understanding beamforming begins with understanding a single sinusoidal wave.*

---

## Introduction

Welcome to the first lesson of the **Beamforming From First Principles Using MATLAB** educational project.

Before studying antenna arrays, phase shifts, time delays, beam steering, or adaptive beamforming algorithms, it is essential to understand the most fundamental signal in engineering: the sinusoidal wave.

This lesson introduces the mathematical model of a sinusoidal signal and demonstrates how to generate and visualize it using MATLAB.

The concepts learned here will be used repeatedly throughout the remainder of this project.

---

## Why This Lesson Matters

Sinusoidal signals appear everywhere in engineering.

They are used to describe:

* Wireless communication signals
* Radar waveforms
* Acoustic waves
* Electromagnetic waves
* Electrical power systems
* Vibrations
* Medical imaging systems
* Beamforming and antenna arrays

A solid understanding of sinusoidal waves makes advanced topics significantly easier to learn.

---

## Learning Question

> **What is a sinusoidal wave, and why is it considered the fundamental building block of communication and radar systems?**

---

## Learning Objectives

After completing this lesson, you should be able to:

* Explain the concept of a sinusoidal waveform.
* Describe the physical meaning of amplitude, frequency, time, and phase.
* Generate a sinusoidal signal in MATLAB.
* Visualize the waveform in the time domain.
* Interpret a basic engineering plot.
