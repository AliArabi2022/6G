## Description

This report accompanies **Lesson 12** of the *Beamforming From First Principles Using MATLAB* educational project.

The report introduces the concept of **wavefronts**, explains their relationship to phase, and demonstrates how wavefronts provide a geometric description of wave propagation. The lesson establishes the foundation for understanding plane waves, antenna arrays, and beamforming.

---

## Learning Objectives

After completing this lesson, readers will be able to:

* Understand the concept of a wavefront.
* Explain the relationship between wavefronts and phase.
* Distinguish between waveforms and wavefronts.
* Interpret circular wavefronts generated by a point source.
* Explain why wavefronts are fundamental in antenna engineering and beamforming.
