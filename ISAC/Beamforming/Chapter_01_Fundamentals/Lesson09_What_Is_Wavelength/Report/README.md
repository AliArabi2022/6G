## Description

This report accompanies **Lesson 09** of the *Beamforming From First Principles Using MATLAB* educational project.

The report introduces the concept of **wavelength**, explains its physical meaning, derives its relationship with frequency and propagation speed, and demonstrates how wavelength influences the spatial behavior of sinusoidal waves.

This lesson marks the transition from time-domain signal analysis to spatial wave analysis and establishes one of the fundamental concepts used throughout antenna engineering and beamforming.

---

## Learning Objectives

After completing this lesson, readers will be able to:

* Understand the physical meaning of wavelength.
* Explain the relationship between wavelength, frequency, and propagation speed.
* Calculate wavelength using the appropriate mathematical equation.
* Interpret sinusoidal waves in the spatial domain.
* Explain why wavelength is fundamental in antenna engineering and array signal processing.

## Educational Philosophy

> **Understand the concept, not memorize the equation.**

This lesson emphasizes conceptual understanding of wavelength and its role in describing propagating waves before introducing more advanced topics such as wave propagation, antenna arrays, and beamforming.
