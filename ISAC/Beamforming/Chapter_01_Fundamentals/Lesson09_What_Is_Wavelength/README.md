# Lesson 09 — What Is Wavelength?

> *Wavelength describes how a wave repeats in space, just as the period describes how it repeats in time.*

---

## Introduction

Welcome to the ninth lesson of the **Beamforming From First Principles Using MATLAB** educational project.

In the previous lessons, we studied sinusoidal signals in the time domain by introducing amplitude, frequency, period, phase, time delay, and angular frequency.

This lesson extends those concepts into the **spatial domain** by introducing **wavelength**, one of the most fundamental quantities in wave propagation, antenna engineering, wireless communications, and beamforming.

---

## Why This Lesson Matters

Wavelength plays a central role in many areas of engineering.

It is used to describe:

* Electromagnetic wave propagation
* Wireless communication systems
* Radar systems
* Antenna arrays
* Beamforming
* Microwave engineering
* Acoustics
* Optics

A solid understanding of wavelength is essential before studying wave propagation and antenna arrays.

---

## Learning Question

> **What is wavelength, and why is it one of the most important quantities in wave propagation and antenna engineering?**

---

## Learning Objectives

After completing this lesson, you should be able to:

* Explain the physical meaning of wavelength.
* Describe the relationship between wavelength, frequency, and propagation speed.
* Calculate wavelength using the appropriate mathematical equation.
* Interpret sinusoidal waves in the spatial domain.
* Explain why antenna spacing is commonly expressed as a fraction of the wavelength.
