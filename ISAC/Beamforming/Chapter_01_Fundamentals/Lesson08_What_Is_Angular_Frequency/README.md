# Lesson 08 — What Is Angular Frequency?

> *Angular frequency describes how fast a waveform rotates through its phase angle, providing the mathematical language used throughout signal processing and beamforming.*

---

## Introduction

Welcome to the eighth lesson of the **Beamforming From First Principles Using MATLAB** educational project.

In previous lessons, sinusoidal signals were described using ordinary frequency measured in hertz (Hz). This lesson introduces **angular frequency**, a mathematically equivalent but more convenient quantity that measures the rate of phase rotation in radians per second.

Angular frequency is one of the most frequently used concepts in signal processing, communications, radar, and beamforming.

---

## Why This Lesson Matters

Angular frequency is fundamental to:

* Signal processing
* Communications
* Radar systems
* Electromagnetic wave analysis
* Fourier analysis
* Complex exponentials
* Beamforming

Understanding angular frequency simplifies many mathematical derivations encountered throughout this project.

---

## Learning Question

> **What is angular frequency, and why is it preferred over ordinary frequency in engineering mathematics?**

---

## Learning Objectives

After completing this lesson, you should be able to:

* Explain the meaning of angular frequency.
* Convert between frequency and angular frequency.
* Interpret angular frequency geometrically.
* Generate sinusoidal signals using angular frequency.
* Understand why angular frequency is widely used in engineering.
