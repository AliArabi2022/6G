## Description

This report accompanies **Lesson 08** of the *Beamforming From First Principles Using MATLAB* educational project.

The report introduces the concept of **angular frequency**, derives its relationship with ordinary frequency, and demonstrates its use in the mathematical representation of sinusoidal signals.

Angular frequency serves as the standard notation used throughout signal processing, communications, electromagnetics, and beamforming.

---

## Learning Objectives

After completing this lesson, readers will be able to:

* Understand the meaning of angular frequency.
* Convert between frequency and angular frequency.
* Explain why angular frequency is preferred in engineering mathematics.
* Generate sinusoidal signals using angular frequency.
* Interpret angular frequency from both mathematical and geometric perspectives.
