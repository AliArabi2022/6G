# References

## Overview

This directory contains the primary references used in developing **Lesson 15 – Wave Superposition**.

The references were selected to ensure scientific accuracy while maintaining an educational approach suitable for students studying wave propagation from first principles.

The lesson combines concepts from wave physics, signals and systems, electromagnetics, and antenna engineering.

---

## Primary References

### 1. French, A. P.

**Vibrations and Waves**

W. W. Norton & Company, 1971.

**Purpose in this lesson**

* Fundamental wave concepts
* Traveling waves
* Principle of Superposition
* Physical interpretation of wave motion

---

### 2. Halliday, D., Resnick, R., & Walker, J.

**Fundamentals of Physics**

11th Edition

John Wiley & Sons, 2018.

**Purpose in this lesson**

* Introduction to mechanical waves
* Wave propagation
* Physical intuition behind wave interactions

---

### 3. Griffiths, D. J.

**Introduction to Electrodynamics**

4th Edition

Cambridge University Press, 2017.

**Purpose in this lesson**

* Electromagnetic wave propagation
* Linear media
* Superposition principle in electromagnetics

---

### 4. Oppenheim, A. V., Willsky, A. S., & Nawab, S. H.

**Signals and Systems**

2nd Edition

Prentice Hall, 1997.

**Purpose in this lesson**

* Linearity
* System response
* Mathematical interpretation of superposition

---

### 5. Haykin, S.

**Communication Systems**

5th Edition

John Wiley & Sons, 2009.

**Purpose in this lesson**

* Communication waveforms
* Carrier signals
* Signal representation

---

### 6. Balanis, C. A.

**Antenna Theory: Analysis and Design**

4th Edition

John Wiley & Sons, 2016.

**Purpose in future lessons**

* Antenna arrays
* Radiation
* Beamforming
* Array factor

Although this reference is not directly required to understand the Principle of Superposition, it provides the theoretical foundation for the beamforming chapters that follow later in the course.

### 7. Mailloux, R. J.

**Phased Array Antenna Handbook**

3rd Edition

Artech House, 2018.

**Purpose in future lessons**

## Reference Usage

The references in this directory support four complementary aspects of the lesson:

* Physical intuition
* Mathematical derivation
* MATLAB implementation
* Future connection to antenna arrays and beamforming

No single reference covers all educational objectives. The lesson integrates concepts from multiple authoritative sources into a unified educational workflow.

---

## Citation Policy

All educational materials developed in this project should cite the original references whenever theoretical concepts, equations, or figures are derived from published sources.

MATLAB figures included in the lesson report are generated by the accompanying simulation code and are fully reproducible.

---

## Notes

This reference collection is intentionally broader than the immediate requirements of Lesson 15.

Several references, particularly those related to antenna arrays and phased-array systems, are included because the Principle of Superposition serves as the theoretical foundation for beamforming, which is the primary focus of this educational project.
