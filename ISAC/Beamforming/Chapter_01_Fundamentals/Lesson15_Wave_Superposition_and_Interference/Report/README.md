## Report Description

This report presents the theoretical background, mathematical foundations, and MATLAB simulations developed for **Lesson 15 – Wave Superposition**.

The report follows the educational philosophy adopted throughout the project:

> **Understand the concept, not memorize the equation.**

Instead of introducing mathematics first, the lesson begins with physical intuition and interactive MATLAB simulations before gradually developing the mathematical description of wave superposition.

The simulations demonstrate that the Principle of Superposition remains valid under different physical conditions, including variations in amplitude, frequency, phase, propagation direction, and waveform shape.

## Educational Objectives

After studying this report, students should be able to:

* Explain the Principle of Superposition.
* Interpret wave interaction using physical intuition.
* Relate the mathematical model to MATLAB simulations.
* Understand why waves preserve their identities after overlap.
* Prepare for subsequent lessons on interference, standing waves, and beamforming.
